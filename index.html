Enemy field guide
  • Grave Husks pursue steadily. Six-legged Gloom Skitters rush faster.
  • Bone Archers keep their distance. Dotted lines warn of fixed-aim arrows.
  • Horned Brutes stop to mark a dashed charge lane, then rush it. Step aside.
  • Mire Hexers mark crossed circles before blight forms. Leave the circle.
  • Crowned elites are swifter (side chevrons) or tougher (square frame). Frost and control still work.
  • Stuns interrupt attacks; roots interrupt charges and stop movement. Released arrows and blight persist after the caster falls.
